What does the nav (and site structure) look like?

You have 4 revenue streams: consulting, community membership, courses, cohort programs. The nav has to make it easy to get to the right thing without overwhelming a first-time visitor.

A — Three Offering Pages

Nav has three clear destinations: Community, Programs (cohort + courses bundled), Consulting. CTA button drives to community join. Clean and conversion-focused.

Best for

  • Simple, scannable nav
  • Courses and cohort feel like one "learning" track

Watch out for

  • Courses might feel buried under "Programs"

B — Named Brands in the Nav

Use the actual product names: The Phan Club, The Roundtable, Work With Me. Builds brand recognition for each offering. People who've heard of "The Phan Club" can find it immediately.

Best for

  • Building product brand equity
  • People referred directly to a specific offering

Watch out for

  • Names mean nothing to a first-time visitor from YouTube

C — Journey-First Nav

"Start Here" becomes a key nav item — a page that explains the framework and directs people to the right offering for where they are. Good for YouTube traffic landing cold on the site.

Best for

  • Cold YouTube traffic who don't know you yet
  • The framework does the selling before they reach a product page

Watch out for

  • Adds a step between visitor and conversion
  • Requires the "Start Here" page to be really good
