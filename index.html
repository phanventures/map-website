The Story

Ambition alone isn't enough. You need the right systems and the right people.

My parents came to America from Vietnam with no English, no safety net, and no guarantee it would work out. They were chasing the American dream from scratch, and they made it happen. That work ethic was in the air I grew up breathing. You don't wait for permission. You go after what you want. And when you fall down — and you will — you pick yourself back up and keep moving.

What I learned later is that drive alone doesn't get you there. Most people with big dreams get stopped by chaos — too many ideas, no clear system, burning out before anything gains traction. What changes everything is building the right workflows, and being around the right people: people who are fearless, who take risks, who show you by example that it's actually possible.

What changed everything for me wasn't just ambition — it was building systems that could carry that ambition forward. Once I stopped winging it and started operating with real workflows, I could run businesses, show up for community, and actually make progress toward the things that mattered. That's what I want to help you build.

The System

The exact framework I use to run 3 businesses.

I run Minnesota Dodgeball, Always Funday, and Phan Ventures — alongside a full-time job. This is the system that makes it possible. Not complicated. Not perfect. Built to actually work.

Get the free guide — the full system, broken down step by step.

01
Capture Everything One inbox — nothing lives in your head. Every task goes into ClickUp, every thought goes into Obsidian. When you have one place to put things, you stop losing them.
02
Weekly Planning Ritual Every Sunday, I plan the week. Thirty minutes that determines what actually gets done versus what gets endlessly pushed. This single habit changed more than any tool ever did.
03
Project-Based Thinking Everything belongs to a project. No floating tasks, no random lists. When every piece of work has a home, nothing gets lost and priorities become obvious.
04
AI-Assisted Execution I use AI to handle repetitive thinking — drafts, planning, research, automations. This frees me to do the work only I can do. Leverage the tools, protect your energy.
05
Regular Review Monthly, I review what's working and what isn't. Systems drift if you don't maintain them. The review is what keeps it honest — and keeps you from sliding back into chaos.
01
Audit Your Current Reality Where is your time and energy actually going? Before you build new systems, you have to see clearly what's running (and draining) you right now.
02
Design Your System Tools, workflows, and rhythms — built for how you actually operate. Not a template you force yourself into. A system that fits your life and your goals.
03
Operations for Solo Operators Running a business without a team. The infrastructure that keeps you moving — without burning out or dropping everything the moment life gets full.
04
Accountability & Peer Learning Weekly group sessions, real feedback, and a cohort of queer entrepreneurs and allies who are building at the same time as you. You're not doing this alone.
